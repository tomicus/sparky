OCHIN Epic vs. Standard Full Epic

Understanding these fundamental architectural and operational differences is key to successful go-live adoption.

Standard "Full" Epic

Typical Single Health System Deployment

  • Isolated Database: Built solely for one healthcare organization. Custom configurations are isolated.
  • Independent Master Files: Provider, medication, and procedure records are unique to the organization.
  • Direct Billing Config: Billing rules tailored to standard commercial, Medicare, and Medicaid fee-for-service.
  • Full Local Build Control: System analysts can modify clinical templates, flowsheets, and forms instantly to match local requests.

OCHIN Collaborative Epic

Shared Safety-Net/FQHC Collaborative Instance

  • Shared Database instance: One instance shared by hundreds of clinics. A patient chart updated at Clinic A is visible at Clinic B in real-time.
  • Shared Master Files: Providers share clinical preference lists, medication files, and order templates to lower maintenance overhead.
  • FQHC & UDS Integration: Heavy focus on sliding scale fees, HRSA federal metrics, and social determinants of health (PRAPARE).
  • Collaborative Decision Making: System customization changes must be reviewed collaboratively to prevent disrupting other members.
