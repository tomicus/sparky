Final Assessment

This 40-question comprehensive exam covers all six training modules. Pass with 80% or higher (32/40) to earn your Ochin Epic Training Certificate of Completion.

⏱ 45 minutes 📋 40 questions 🎯 Pass: 80% (32/40) ↺ Unlimited retakes

📚 What This Exam Covers

🏥
Module 1 — Ochin Overview & Login
Ochin vs. standard Epic, environments, login & 2FA, Hyperspace navigation, In Basket basics
🪪
Module 2 — Patient Check-In
Patient search, new registration, insurance verification, sliding fee scale, check-in workflow, consent, walk-ins/no-shows
📝
Module 3 — Clinical Documentation
Chart navigation, SOAP notes, SmartPhrases, flowsheets & vitals, problem list, signing & cosigning, AVS
💊
Module 4 — Medications & Rx
Medication reconciliation, ordering, e-prescribing, drug alerts, controlled substances & PDMP, refills, 340B
🔬
Module 5 — Orders & Results
Lab & imaging orders, result review, critical values, In Basket management, referrals, prior auth, care gaps
📅
Modules 6 & 7 — Scheduling, Billing & Go-Live
Scheduling, FQHC billing, UDS, telehealth, Ochin-specific workflows, go-live day scenarios

📋 Exam Rules

  • You have 45 minutes to complete all 40 questions — a countdown timer will run at the top of the screen
  • All questions are single-answer multiple choice
  • You can review and change answers before submitting
  • After submission, all answers are revealed with explanations
  • You must score 32 out of 40 (80%) to pass and earn your certificate
  • If you do not pass, review the feedback and retake — there is no limit on attempts
  • After passing, enter your name to generate a printable Certificate of Completion
💡 Before You Begin: Make sure you have completed all training modules (Parts 1–7). The exam covers material from every section. If you feel unsure about a topic, go back to that part and review before starting the exam.
