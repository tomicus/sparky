Ochin Overview & Logging In

Understand how Ochin Epic differs from standard Epic, navigate the environment, and log in securely using your credentials and two-factor authentication.

⏱ ~20 min 🎯 Clinicians · Providers · All Staff 📋 5 sections · Quiz at end

🌐 What Is Ochin?

Ochin (formerly known as Oregon Community Health Information Network) is a nonprofit health IT organization that serves Federally Qualified Health Centers (FQHCs), Rural Health Clinics (RHCs), and safety-net providers across the United States. Ochin operates a shared Epic instance — meaning hundreds of community health organizations share a single Epic environment managed centrally by Ochin.

This is fundamentally different from a hospital system that purchases and self-hosts Epic. When you work at an Ochin member clinic, your Epic is configured, updated, and supported by Ochin's central team, not your local IT department.

🔑 Key Concept: Because Ochin manages a shared Epic platform, your clinic shares database infrastructure with other community health centers. Patient records are segmented by organization — patients at Clinic A cannot be seen by staff at Clinic B — but the system, build, and workflows are standardized across the network.

Why Ochin Exists

  • FQHCs and small safety-net clinics cannot typically afford to license and operate their own Epic instance
  • Ochin negotiates group pricing and provides Epic expertise that individual clinics lack
  • Centralized management means consistent upgrades, security patches, and regulatory compliance (UDS reporting, HRSA requirements)
  • Ochin maintains specialized content for FQHC workflows: sliding fee scales, grant reporting, UDS data capture

🖥️ Epic Environments: Production, Training & Playground

Ochin maintains separate Epic environments. Knowing which one you're in is critical — mistakes in Production affect real patients.

The Three Environments

  1. Production (PRD / PROD) This is the LIVE environment with real patient data. All clinical care happens here. Never practice or test in Production.

    🔴 LIVE DATA Real Patients
  2. Training (TRN) A copy of the Production environment with fictional training patients. Used for onboarding and practice. Refreshed periodically by Ochin. Accessible via a separate URL or Citrix application labeled "Training."

    🟡 FAKE DATA Safe to Practice
  3. Playground / Sandbox (PLY) Used by Ochin analysts for build and testing. Clinic staff generally do not have access to this environment.
⚠️ Warning: The Training environment banner is usually labeled at the top of the screen with an orange or yellow bar. Always verify you are in the correct environment before documenting on a patient chart.

🔐 Logging In to Ochin Epic

Ochin Epic is typically accessed via Citrix Virtual Desktop or a direct web browser connection depending on your organization's setup. Your login credentials are assigned by your clinic's Epic Security Coordinator or Ochin directly.

Step-by-Step Login Process

  1. Launch Citrix Workspace or Browser Open Citrix Workspace app on your workstation, or navigate to your clinic's Epic web URL (e.g., https://hyperspace.ochin.org). Your IT coordinator will provide the exact URL.
  2. Enter Your EMP ID and Password Your EMP ID (Employee ID) is your Epic username. This is NOT your email address. Format is typically your clinic's prefix followed by your assigned number. Example: GVCH0042
  3. Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Ochin requires 2FA for all production access. You will be prompted to approve via the Duo Security mobile app, or enter a one-time code. Approve the push notification on your phone within 60 seconds.
  4. Select Your Role / Context If you hold multiple roles (e.g., you are both a provider and a department coordinator), Epic may prompt you to select which context to open. Choose your primary role for this session.
  5. Review and Dismiss HIPAA Attestation A compliance message may appear requiring you to click Accept acknowledging you understand your HIPAA responsibilities before proceeding.
  6. Your Epic Desktop (Hyperspace) Opens You will land on your personalized Epic Hyperspace homepage showing your schedule, In Basket, and patient list depending on your role.
💡 Pro Tip — Windows Authentication: If your clinic uses Single Sign-On (SSO) through Windows Active Directory, your Windows desktop login may automatically pass your credentials into Epic. You may only see the 2FA prompt rather than a username/password screen.

Password Policy

  • Minimum 12 characters, mix of uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character
  • Password expires every 90 days — you'll receive warnings at 14 days and 7 days
  • Do not share your password — every action in Epic is logged with your EMP ID
  • Forgotten password: contact your Security Coordinator or call the Ochin Help Desk at the number posted at your workstation
⚠️ Never share your Epic login. Every order, documentation entry, and chart access is permanently associated with your credentials. Shared logins are an HIPAA violation and grounds for termination.

Key Navigation Areas

  • Epic Button (⚡) — Top-left. Opens the main menu with access to all Epic modules, recent patients, and settings.
  • Toolbar — Top row. Contains global navigation: Patient Search, Schedule, In Basket (your messages), Reporting.
  • Activity Tabs — Appear when you open a patient chart. Each tab is a section of the chart (Chart, Orders, Results, etc.).
  • Workspace — The large center area showing current content.
  • Patient Header — When in a chart, shows key patient info at the top: name, DOB, MRN, allergies, insurance.

Ochin Epic Glossary

Terms you'll hear every day in Ochin Epic. More terms added in each Part.

A–F

AVS (After Visit Summary)
Patient education printout generated at the end of a visit. Required for Meaningful Use.
BPA (Best Practice Advisory)
Pop-up alerts in Epic reminding providers of clinical guidelines (e.g., "Due for flu shot").
CDS (Clinical Decision Support)
Automated alerts and suggestions built into Epic to guide clinical decisions.
EHR (Electronic Health Record)
The digital patient chart. Epic is the EHR Ochin uses.
EMP ID (Employee ID)
Your unique Epic username assigned by your Security Coordinator.
FQHC (Federally Qualified Health Center)
Community health centers receiving federal funding under Section 330 of the Public Health Service Act. Most Ochin members are FQHCs.

H–M

Hyperspace
Epic's main desktop application interface — the main window you use every day.
In Basket
Epic's internal inbox for messages, results notifications, refill requests, and tasks.
MAR (Medication Administration Record)
The section of Epic tracking medications ordered and administered during a visit or inpatient stay.
MRN (Medical Record Number)
Unique patient identifier in Epic. Used to pull up a patient's chart.
MyChart
Epic's patient portal. Patients can view results, message providers, and request appointments.

N–Z

PRD / PROD
Production environment — the live system with real patient data.
RHC (Rural Health Clinic)
Federally designated rural clinic eligible for enhanced Medicare/Medicaid reimbursement. Many Ochin members are RHCs.
SmartPhrase / Dot Phrase
Shortcuts in Epic's note editor. Type a period followed by a keyword to auto-expand to a full text template. E.g., ".hpi" expands to a History of Present Illness template.
TRN
Training environment — safe to practice with fictional patient data.
UDS (Uniform Data System)
Annual federal reporting requirement for FQHCs to HRSA. Ochin Epic is built to capture and report UDS data automatically.
